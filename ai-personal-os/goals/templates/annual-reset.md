# Annual Reset — a one-day restart protocol

> Once a year (or when you've accumulated a "sick of spinning in place" feeling). One full day:
> morning — excavation, afternoon — breaking autopilot, evening — synthesis.
> Goal: move through **dissonance → uncertainty → discovery** so clarity cuts through distraction.
> You need pen, paper, and an open mind. The morning excavation is **AI-free** (break the thinking
> limiter yourself first).

**Where results land (don't duplicate — write directly to the source):**
- Anti-vision → `IWS/self/anti-vision.md`
- Identity statements → `IWS/self/identity.md`
- 1-year lens → `goals/yearly.md`
- 1-month project → `goals/monthly.md`
- Daily levers → `goals/daily.md` / `goals/weekly.md`
- Constraints → `goals/values.md`
- Vision MVP → `goals/vision.md`

---

## Part 1. Morning — Psychological excavation (15–30 min, on paper)

**The pain (anti-vision, entry point):**
1. The dull, constant dissatisfaction you've learned to live with?
2. What do you complain about but don't change? (3 frequent complaints from the past year)
3. For each: what would an observer conclude about what you *actually* want from your behavior?
4. What truth about your life would be unbearable to admit in front of someone you respect?

**Anti-vision (average Tuesday if nothing changes):** 5 years → 10 years → end of life.
**Identity:** what identity to let go of? The most embarrassing reason? What are you protecting and at what cost?
→ write into `IWS/self/anti-vision.md`

**Vision MVP (3 years from now, without regard to realism):** average Tuesday in detail.
- What would you need to believe about yourself for this to feel natural? → "I am someone who…" statement into `IWS/self/identity.md`
- One action this week, if you were already that person?

---

## Part 2. Afternoon — Breaking autopilot

> Set reminders/events on your phone with the question inside the notification. The more random the
> timing, the better. Set them for walking / commuting / idle moments.

- 11:00 — What am I currently avoiding by doing what I'm doing?
- 13:30 — If someone filmed the last 2 hours, what would they conclude about what I want from life?
- 15:15 — Am I moving toward the life I hate, or toward the one I want?
- 17:00 — What important thing am I pretending doesn't matter?
- 19:30 — What did I do today from identity protection, not genuine desire?
- 21:00 — When did I feel alive today? When dead?

Additional (for walks / commute):
- What changes if I stop needing to be seen as [identity from Part 1]?
- Where in life am I trading aliveness for safety?
- What's the smallest version of the person I want to become that's available tomorrow?

---

## Part 3. Evening — Synthesis

1. After today — what's the most honest thing about why I'm stuck?
2. Who is the real enemy? Name it clearly — not circumstances, not people, but an internal pattern/belief.
3. One sentence: what I refuse to allow my life to become. → anchor in `anti-vision.md`
4. One sentence: what I'm building toward (will evolve). → `vision.md`

---

## After the reset

- Archive this session's notes in `IWS/reflections/YYYY-MM-DD-annual-reset.md`
- Update `goals/vision.md` and `goals/yearly.md` based on what emerged
- Start `/monthly-review` to translate the synthesis into a concrete first month
