# Profile

Your background and résumé. Claude reads this when drafting messages, applications, cover letters,
pitches, or anything that should reflect who you are and what you've done.

- `profile-template.md` — fill this in with your real background, then it becomes your source of
  truth for outbound writing.

> Keep the real, detailed version private if it contains personal data — add it to `.gitignore`
> like the other private folders.
